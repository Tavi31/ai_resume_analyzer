Machine Learning Engineer

We are seeking a Machine Learning Engineer to join our analytics team.
The ideal candidate has strong experience in Python, deep learning frameworks
such as TensorFlow or PyTorch, and a solid understanding of data preprocessing
and model deployment. Familiarity with cloud environments (AWS or GCP) and
version control (Git) is a plus.

Responsibilities:
- Develop and deploy machine learning models for production.
- Collaborate with data scientists and engineers to optimize data pipelines.
- Ensure scalability and efficiency of model inference systems.

Qualifications:
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field.
- 2+ years of hands-on experience with machine learning projects.
- Strong programming and analytical skills.
